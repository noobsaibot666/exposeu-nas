# Recommended wording rules

Use:

**Release Content Kit**

Explain it as:

**A photo and video package for artists preparing a single, album, show, tour, or campaign release.**

Avoid leading with:

- media press
- media press kit
- content solution
- visual assets package

These sound either unclear, too corporate, or too technical.

EPK can be mentioned later in the page:

**Also useful for EPKs, press releases, booking profiles, and social media campaigns.**
