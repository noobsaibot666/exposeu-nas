# Meta Ads Campaign — expose.u

## Campaign Setup

**Geo:** Berlin + Germany. Hard targeting. No audience expansion.
**Objective:** Lead generation (contact form submission)
**Optimization event:** Lead (fires on contact form success)
**Pixel events confirmed:** PageView → ViewContent → LandingPageCtaClick → Lead

**UTM base structure:**
```
utm_source=meta
utm_medium=paid_social
utm_campaign=<see per-page below>
utm_content=<ad-set or creative label>
```
